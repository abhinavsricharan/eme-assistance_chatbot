# eme-assistance_chatbot

Overview
The Emergency Assistance Chatbot is designed to provide quick and reliable support during natural disasters and emergencies. It shares emergency contact numbers, safety tips, and shelter/evacuation locations across all states in India. The chatbot helps users make faster, safer decisions in critical situations.

Features
📞 Provide emergency contact numbers (police, ambulance, fire, disaster management).
🛡️ Share safety tips for disasters (earthquake, flood, cyclone, etc.).
🏠 Offer shelter and evacuation location details.
🌍 Respond to real-time and localized queries.

Development Process
Planning & Research – Defined objectives, collected state-wise emergency data.
Design – Built conversational flow and structured knowledge base.
Development – Implemented chatbot logic and integrated state-wise shelters.
Testing – Checked functionality, usability, and scalability.
Deployment & Maintenance – Deployed for use and set up regular data updates.

Example Interaction
Welcome to the Emergency Assistance Chatbot!
You can ask about emergency contacts, safety tips, or shelter locations.
Type 'exit' to quit the chatbot.

You: What is the ambulance number?
Chatbot: The ambulance helpline number is 102.

You: Where are flood shelters in Kolkata?
Chatbot: Shelters in West Bengal for floods: Salt Lake Stadium, Rabindra Bharati University.
